# Naming

- Build the vocabulary from the changed code, its callers, and owning
  documentation. Flag a name when it makes the concept, responsibility, or
  behavior harder to identify, not merely because another name is possible.
- Look for invented terms without a clear meaning, unexplained jargon or
  shorthand, vague names that conceal important distinctions, and multiple
  names for the same concept. Check whether one name means different things in
  different places.
- Distinguish related concepts from accidental synonyms. Prefer the smallest
  vocabulary change that makes the concept consistent across code, comments,
  and protocol boundaries.
- Support findings with the unclear occurrences, the concept they refer to,
  and the reader or maintenance consequence. Do not manufacture a finding when
  vocabulary is clear and consistent.
