Dragging a tab to split

Drag a tab over a group. Dropping on the left, right, top or bottom quarter splits the group that way, and the shaded area shows where the new group will go. Dropping in the middle or on a tab strip moves the tab into that group. This is VS Code's editor drop behavior.
